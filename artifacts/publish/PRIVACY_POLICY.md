# Privacy Policy (DRAFT — requires legal review before release)

_Last updated: [date]_

**Screen Recorder** ("the App") runs entirely on your computer.

## What the App records
Screen, microphone, system audio and webcam content are recorded only when you press Record. Recordings are saved to the folder you choose and are never uploaded by the App.

## Data stored locally
- Settings (`%APPDATA%\ScreenRecorder\settings.json`)
- Licence key, if you activate Pro (`%APPDATA%\ScreenRecorder\license.key`)
- Diagnostic logs and crash reports (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ScreenRecorder\logs`), kept 14 days. They contain app version, Windows version, and technical error text; not recording content.

## Data sent over the network
- **Crash reports** — only if you enable "Send crash reports" in About. A report contains the exception text, app version and Windows version. Off by default.
- **Update check** — if enabled (default), the App downloads a small manifest from [update server] to see if a newer version exists. Your IP address is visible to that server as with any web request. No other data is sent.
- Licence validation is performed offline; no data is sent.

## Your choices
You can disable crash reports and update checks in About, export or delete logs at any time, and uninstall the App. Uninstalling leaves your recordings and settings in place.

## Your responsibility
Recording other people (e.g. in calls or meetings) may require their consent under local law. You are responsible for lawful use.
